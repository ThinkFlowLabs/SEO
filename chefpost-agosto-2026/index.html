Mobile is where the change shows most clearly. Mobile click rate went from 1.39 percent in July to 3.01 percent in August, which is more than double, on 24 percent more impressions. The image and speed work described below landed in the middle of the month, and mobile is where page weight is felt.

September is running ahead so far. The first six days produced 131 clicks at a 2.06 percent click rate. Six days is not a trend, and we would not forecast off it, but the direction has held through the start of the month.

The encouraging half
