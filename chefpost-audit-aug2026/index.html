01 · Executive Summary

The verdict in 60 seconds.

The July rebuild did exactly what a launch is supposed to do in its first three weeks: every technical bug from May is fixed, 38 of 40 pages are indexed and earning impressions, and AI assistants now recommend Chefpost six times out of twenty where they used to say its name once. What has not moved yet is clicks, and the reasons are no longer on-page.

  1. Every May-critical bug is verifiably fixed. Blog posts self-canonical, the homepage and /filter carry keyword H1s, all 40 new pages emit five valid JSON-LD blocks (169 of 169 items validate, invalid schema went 38 to 0), and dev./booking. subdomains are gone from DNS entirely.
  2. AI search flipped first. The identical 5-prompt × 4-model test scored 6 of 20 citations, versus 1 of 20 in May, and every cited URL is a July page: the Miami page, the cost guide, the Hamptons page, the yacht page. The 90-day target was 3.
  3. Google is serving the pages but not yet paying them. The cost guide sits at position 6.75 with 2,659 impressions, and neighborhoods rank top-10, but total clicks are 7.6/day vs 6.4 pre-launch. Non-brand CTR is 0.12%; brand still carries 86% of clicks.
  4. The bottleneck moved from content to authority and measurement. Referring domains are flat at 207 (the only new links are spam shorteners), Authority Score is stuck at 16 vs takeachef's 43, there is still no local-pack presence, and GA4 has zero key events configured, so none of this can be tied to bookings yet.
  5. The next 90 days are about fuel, not pages: conversion tracking, host consolidation, two cannibalization merges, GBP execution, the seasonal cluster before Sept 1, and a real link program. Each is specified below.

Our verdict

The May diagnosis was correct and the July prescription is taking effect in the right order: technical first, indexation second, AI citations third, rankings currently in the volatile entry phase, clicks last. Nothing in the new data says the strategy is wrong; everything says it is under-fueled.

Three things now decide whether this compounds or stalls: links (207 flat referring domains cannot hold positions 11 to 48 against a 2,486-domain leader), the local pack (the Miami money SERPs are won in Maps, where Chefpost still does not appear), and measurement (zero GA4 key events means zero provable ROI).

Confidence: high on the direction, moderate on the timeline. Rankings 2 to 4 weeks post-launch are violently volatile; the three data sources in this report disagree about individual positions but agree on every structural conclusion.

· · ·
1
CRITICAL · MEASUREMENT

The program is flying blind: zero key events, and a fake GA4 property still collecting.

Impact: no conversion data on 15,800+ YTD sessions; every SEO decision from here is unprovable.

GA4 property G-41JH1G30DC records zero key events across all channels year-to-date. Simultaneously, the placeholder property G-12345HHDSD, flagged in May, still fires on the homepage through GTM-WZV66CS next to the real tag, splitting hits into a dead property. Semrush independently reports the site audit's GA connection as NOT_CONNECTED.

Fix: In GTM: delete the placeholder gtag destination. In GA4: define 4 key events, chef_contact (profile contact/quote), filter_engaged (filter search with location), whatsapp_click (wa.me tap), booking_start. Half a day of work; it turns every other number in this report into revenue math.
2
CRITICAL · AUTHORITY

The link profile did not move, and what did move is spam.

Impact: pages enter at positions 11 to 48 and stall; the leader holds 2,486 referring domains to Chefpost's 207.

Since July: referring domains 210 → 207 (Semrush), and every link DataForSEO logged in June-August is a URL shortener or scraper with spam score 50 to 65. Only five linking domains have authority 50+. Meanwhile the SERPs the new pages entered are cheap to win, "how much does a private chef cost" top-10 pages average 1.8 referring domains, "private chef miami" just 1.7, which is precisely why a real link program has outsized returns here.

Fix: Run the 4-tier program in section 08: citations first (Yelp, Bing, Apple, chambers), then STR/yacht/wedding partnerships, then digital PR anchored on a proprietary-stats page. 90-day goal: +30 to +40 real referring domains, AS 16 → 20.
3
HIGH · CANNIBALIZATION

Chefpost is its own strongest competitor on its two best clusters.

Impact: Miami and cost, the two highest-value clusters, each split signals across 2 to 3 URLs.

Miami: the old blog guide ranks pos ~13 for "private chefs miami" while /private-chef-miami sits at pos 33; /filter also ranks for Miami intent. Semrush tracking shows the symptom: "miami private chef" fell 14 → 37 while "private chef miami fl" entered at 32, classic URL-flip churn. Cost: the old post /blog/how-much-is-a-personal-chef-less-than-you-think still ranks pos 24-35 on the 3,600-volume cluster that /private-chef-cost (pos 6.75 in GSC) is built for.

Fix: 301 the old cost post into /private-chef-cost. In Sanity, edit the Miami blog guide to link prominently to /private-chef-miami (and reference it as the canonical resource). Add /private-chef-miami links from homepage and footer Miami mentions. Do not delete the blog guide; repoint its equity.
4
HIGH · LOCAL

The money SERPs are won in the map pack, where Chefpost still is not.

Impact: "private chef near me" + "personal chef near me" = 16,200 monthly searches at KD 1, served by a pack Chefpost never enters.

Live SERP checks confirm: Miami, services and near-me queries lead with a local 3-pack above organic #1. Chefpost's GBP (5.0 stars, 72 reviews) has the thinnest category set in the market, 36 of 37 services with empty descriptions, a description naming zero cities, ~0.7 reviews/month velocity, and zero reviews mentioning a service or city. Competitors rank in the pack from twice the distance with fewer reviews. The full paste-ready punch list already exists (GBP-8-PROMPT-STACK-JUL2026.md) and management access was granted Jul 27.

Fix: Execute the GBP doc: description v3, 12 service texts, categories (+Service establishment, +Catering food and drink supplier, +Food and beverage consultant), onsite-services attribute, weekly posts, and a review-request flow that prompts city + service mentions. No new research needed; it is written and waiting.
5
STRUCTURAL · CTR

AI Overviews are absorbing the clicks the cost cluster earns, and legacy hosts leak the rest.

Impact: position 6.75 with 0.3% CTR on the cost guide; 17% of all clicks still landing on http:// and www. URLs.

The cost and sushi SERPs open with an AI Overview; "how much does a private chef cost" at position 8.8 yields zero clicks in GSC. That makes AEO the economically correct answer, and it is already working: GPT-5.5 quotes the cost guide's prices verbatim. Separately, the http:// homepage still collects 71 clicks / 13,788 impressions at position 4.0, and www. chef profiles (francesco-nyc: 22 clicks) rank on the wrong host, signals split four ways since May.

Fix: Server-level 301s: http → https and www → apex, one redirect rule each. Keep dollar figures in the first screen of every money page (they are what every LLM quoted). Sharpen titles on pos-6-to-15 pages toward click-worthy formats ("2026 Price Guide" is already the cited pattern).

Methodology
  • GSC: last-3-months export (May 2 to Aug 1, 2026, Web search, domain property: Queries, Pages, Chart, Devices, Countries), contrasted against the May 29 and June 12 16-month exports. Launch split at July 15.
  • GA4: Traffic acquisition by default channel group, Jan 1 to Aug 3, 2026 (property chefpost.com, account Summerlee).
  • Semrush (Aug 2-3): domain overview, organic research, backlinks, position tracking campaign 29801289, site audit (health 82, 227 pages).
  • DataForSEO (Aug 3): Labs ranked keywords (298), backlinks, competitor domains, Lighthouse (desktop form factor this run), and 5 live mobile SERP pulls for the money keywords.
  • Playwright live crawl (Aug 3): 15 templates, full-scroll at 1440px, homepage also 375px; extraction of canonicals, H1s, JSON-LD, alts, word counts, analytics tags; DNS checks on dev./booking. subdomains.
  • Flagship LLM citation test (Aug 3): 5 prompts × 4 models = 20 live calls via DataForSEO AI Optimization, web_search enabled on every call. Models: Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro (preview), GPT-5.5, Sonar-Reasoning-Pro. The verbatim prompts:
    1. "Best private chef booking platform in Miami"
    2. "How do I hire a private chef for a dinner party in the Hamptons?"
    3. "Private chef meal prep service in New York City"
    4. "How much does a private chef cost in Florida?"
    5. "Yacht chef services in Miami"
    A citation = Chefpost named in the answer text, or a chefpost.com URL cited as a source the answer used. Source-pool-only appearances (one, on the NYC prompt) were not counted.

Limitations, read before quoting numbers
  • 18 days is not a rankings verdict. The launch window is inside Google's re-ranking churn. The three sources disagree on individual positions (Semrush Aug 2 shows sushi top-10 and cost #11-18; DataForSEO Aug 3 shows those slots vacated; GSC's 3-month average shows cost at 6.75). We report all three and anchor conclusions only where they agree.
  • GSC page rows aggregate the full 3-month window, so per-page average positions mix strong and weak weeks.
  • Lighthouse ran desktop form factor this cycle (MCP limitation); the May baseline was mobile. LCP comparisons are directional.
  • GA4 has no key events, so all business-impact statements are inferences from engagement, not conversions, which is precisely why finding #1 is finding #1.
  • The LLM grid is a 20-call sample. Model answers vary run to run; the frozen prompt set limits, but does not remove, that variance. Monthly re-runs build the trendline that single runs cannot.
