Why we built these

The demand was already there. There was nowhere to send it.

Chefpost's own Google Search Console showed the site being surfaced for city and service searches across 16 months — but with no dedicated landing pages, that demand landed on the generic /filter query URLs, which converted at just 0.2%–0.5% CTR from page 3–5 of Google.

The May 2026 SEO audit named it directly: priority #4 was "Build static city landing pages", starting with Miami. Non-brand organic CTR sat at 0.32%, and 79.5% of all clicks were branded — the marketplace was barely acquiring customers from non-brand search.

So we sized the real opportunity with DataForSEO and Semrush, then built a page for each pocket of demand. The table on the right is the demand Google was already showing Chefpost, with no page to catch it.

The gap in one line: Google was already showing Chefpost ~14,799 impressions/16mo for Miami private-chef terms at position ~25–45 — with no Miami page. Now there is one, plus 38 more.
