01 / The month's outcome

More useful content, with a cleaner foundation underneath.

The work covered both what customers see and what search engines receive. Existing pages became easier to read and use. New pages connected specific needs to a relevant chef, inquiry or browsing path. Shared templates gained cleaner URLs, metadata and structured data, so improvements extend across the marketplace instead of being patched one page at a time.

We also changed how progress is measured. Accepted inquiries, telephone clicks and chat intent now have defined roles. Page weight is measured with complete mobile reports. Every major release was checked in production, with limitations retained alongside the results.

How to read this report

Completed work is separated from proven business outcomes.

Published pages and passing checks do not establish Google indexing, new bookings or a passing mobile experience. This is a September work report, not a claim of finalized full-month traffic or revenue growth. Earlier pages improved this month are not counted as new launches.

05 / Technical SEO

Repaired the shared chef and offer templates.

  • URL consolidation: standardized service-path casing and redirected alternate representations to stored canonical identities. The fix preserves real chef/offer slugs rather than blindly lowercasing every identifier.
  • Metadata and entities: added self-canonicals, useful titles/descriptions, breadcrumbs and profile/person/service/offer structured data to the marketplace templates. Corrected empty titles, missing H1s and the React-style title artifact.
  • Price semantics: corrected the case-dependent meal-prep unit display, including the inspected Gina offer showing a session price. This does not claim a new backend-wide pricing model.
  • Robots, sitemaps and errors: verified crawl-control endpoints, normalized sitemap generation around a validated same-origin inventory adapter, removed inappropriate entries and retired demo routes. Genuine missing records return 404; temporary backend failures return 503 instead of misleading homepage redirects.
  • Public page data: limited serialized chef/profile data to fields the public interface needs, preserving booking/menu types. Also reduced homepage fields and replaced serialized authentication values with a boolean. A complete backend exposure review remains separate.
  • Business identity: centralized Organization/WebSite references and linked website schema to the existing verified Google Business Profile through Maps and Place ID. Aligned publisher/social identifiers across templates. This was website integration, not creation of a new Business Profile.

08 / Research and prioritization

Used search and analytics evidence to decide what to work on.

  • Boston and regional demand: assessed Boston, Cape Cod, Nantucket and related markets against search demand, competitors and actual platform supply. Distinguished potential search demand from social or referral leads.
  • City-page discovery: investigated New Jersey, New York and Austin visibility, including filtered URLs competing with the intended commercial pages. Prioritized canonical signals, internal discovery and useful customer routes.
  • Seasonal demand: compared Halloween and adjacent holiday searches through DataForSEO and Semrush. Selected “halloween dinner party ideas” and connected the information need to optional chef hiring.
  • Pages still receiving search attention: compared Search Console landing-page clicks and impressions with the pages already changed, producing a backlog grounded in existing visibility rather than guesswork.
  • Blog versus newsletter: compared the native blog and Substack in Search Console and checked representative Google-selected canonicals before setting their publishing roles.
  • Growth and lead measurement: produced a practical growth plan and contact-measurement specification, with page/channel reporting and a clear distinction between traffic, intent and actual contacts.
  • Summerlee reconciliation: checked the external audit against live behavior, source code, fresh crawl data, GSC, GA4, Semrush and DataForSEO. Documented confirmed findings, superseded observations, unresolved engineering work and acceptance criteria.
  • Authority and AI discovery: refreshed competitor/backlink context and inspected AI-referral evidence. Kept GA4 assistant referrals separate from controlled AI-answer visibility, and corrected earlier wording that conflated brand mentions with verified citations.

Research, comparison reports and recommendations are completed deliverables. They are not evidence that an outreach campaign, new backlinks, an AI booking interface or additional rankings were delivered. Provider authority scores are not interchangeable, and older AI prompt panels are not reproduced by referral traffic.

05 / SEO técnico

Corregimos las plantillas compartidas de chefs y servicios.

  • Consolidación de URLs: normalizamos mayúsculas de rutas de servicio y redirigimos variantes a las identidades canónicas almacenadas. Conservamos los identificadores reales de chefs y ofertas, sin convertirlos indiscriminadamente a minúsculas.
  • Metadatos y entidades: añadimos canónicas propias, títulos y descripciones útiles, migas de pan y datos estructurados de perfiles, personas, servicios y ofertas. Corregimos títulos vacíos, H1 faltantes y el artefacto de React dentro del título.
  • Unidades de precio: corregimos la etiqueta de meal prep que dependía de las mayúsculas, incluida la oferta revisada de Gina con precio por sesión. No equivale a implementar un nuevo modelo de precios en todo el backend.
  • Robots, sitemaps y errores: verificamos los puntos de control de rastreo, normalizamos la generación del sitemap mediante un adaptador de inventario validado, retiramos entradas incorrectas y páginas de demostración. Los registros inexistentes responden 404 y los fallos temporales del backend 503, en lugar de redirigir a la portada.
  • Datos públicos: limitamos los datos serializados de perfiles a los campos necesarios, conservando los tipos usados por menús y reservas. Reducimos también los datos de portada y sustituimos valores de autenticación por un booleano. La exposición del backend requiere una revisión aparte.
  • Identidad del negocio: centralizamos Organization/WebSite y vinculamos el esquema con el Google Business Profile verificado existente, mediante Maps y Place ID. Alineamos referencias editoriales y sociales. No creamos un Business Profile nuevo.
