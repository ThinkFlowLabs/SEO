01 · Executive Summary

The answer in 60 seconds.

The instinct behind this brief is correct, and the data is unusually one-sided: every measurement we ran says this category is rising, unclaimed, and winnable with content Chefpost is uniquely positioned to write.

  1. The trend is real and structural, not a spike. US search interest in "supper club" is at its all-time high, up 45% in five years, with peaks every November and December. "Founder dinner" registered its first-ever Google Trends signal in 2026. "Private chef" interest has grown 160% in five years. Diana is early, not late.
  2. Nobody owns it. Across twelve target searches, page one belongs to Reddit threads, Medium posts, Substack newsletters and personal blogs from 2016 to 2018. The biggest competitor, Take a Chef, ranks #1 on every "hire a chef" query using generic city pages and has zero supper-club, founder, corporate or influencer content. A blog that launched in January 2026 already reached #7 on "how to start a supper club". Entry is fast here.
  3. Chefpost already holds the beachhead. A blog post from April 2024 ranks position 1 today for "what is a supper club", "how to start a supper club with friends" and five sibling queries, with a generic title tag and no internal structure. The head query "what is a supper club" carries 5,400 searches per month at keyword difficulty 5. The post is ranking on trust alone; it has never been optimized.
  4. AI search is where the category will actually be decided. "Supper club" gets 16,411 AI-assistant searches per month, three times any other term we tested. Today the AI answers cite Wikipedia, Reddit and Instagram reels. Chefpost's entire AI footprint is its pricing page (26 recorded citations, all cost questions). The mechanism that earned those citations, concrete numbers on exact-phrase pages, extends directly to this category.
  5. The engine needs two repairs while we build. A new redirect misconfiguration sends every www visitor to a dead server port (this is the "site feels slow" complaint from our Aug 5 call), and GA4 has still recorded zero conversions. Both fixes are specified in Section 02 and cost hours, not weeks.

Our recommendation

Claim the category in three moves: (1) upgrade the already-ranking 2024 post into a true Supper Club pillar this month, (2) publish the occasion pages nobody has built (founder dinners, brand and influencer dinners, client appreciation dinners, corporate dinner chef), each with a price table, and (3) pair every page with Diana's PR run so the same phrases appear in interviews, backlinks and AI answers.

The search volume on the founder-dinner side is still small. That is precisely the point: the category vocabulary is forming right now, and the pages that define it will be the ones AI assistants quote when the volume arrives. Supper clubs supply today's traffic; founder dinners supply the land grab.

Confidence: high. Four independent data sources (Ads volumes, five-year Trends, live SERPs, live LLM answers) agree on every structural conclusion.
